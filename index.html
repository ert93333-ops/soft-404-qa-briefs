FAQ

Soft 404 constraints are explicit.

Does it crawl my site?

No. This MVP only analyzes pasted URL/status/title/body rows, owner notes, and redirect hop counts.

Does it replace Search Console or a crawler?

No. Search Console and crawler suites remain necessary. This is a fast prelaunch sample review and handoff tool.

Does it guarantee indexing?

No. It produces launch QA guidance and does not guarantee crawling, indexing, rankings, or Search Console outcomes.

Can I paste private client URLs?

The app is browser-local, but you should still paste only public-safe or anonymized samples.
